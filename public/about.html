About nothing.

nothing. is a one-page interactive art piece built around a single choice. You are offered two doors: see nothing, which opens an entirely white room, or see everything, which costs a symbolic ₹1 and opens a dark room holding a single word.

It is a small experiment about expectation: how much a person will pay, or hold back, for something that is described only by its name. There is nothing hidden behind either door, and nothing to download or sign up for.

How the site is funded

The ₹1 unlock and the advertisement on the landing page help cover hosting and keep the project online. Ads are served by Google AdSense; see the privacy policy for details on cookies and data.
